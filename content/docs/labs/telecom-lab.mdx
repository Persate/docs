**Telecom Lab** is a map-based lab. It gives a versioned view of network deployment, service availability, demand, infrastructure feasibility, institutional readiness and data coverage in every municipality in Poland. Each municipality is a separate unit of analysis, and all results come from a single reviewed data release.

Telecom Lab is available only to organizations and groups it is assigned to. When assigned, it appears by name in the **Labs** section of the sidebar.

## Tabs and views

The tab strip has two tabs:

* **Summary** — what the lab covers, the analysis dimensions, the sources and their coverage, how to read the results and the date of the data;
* **Municipality map** — the interactive map.

At the right end of the strip, a switch toggles the map between **Analysis view** and **General view**, and the Advisor button opens the main **Advisor**.

## Analysis view

The analysis view offers six modes, chosen in the sidebar:

| Mode | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Ranking** | Rank municipalities across five outcome areas. |
| **Evidence** | Map one source measure at a time. |
| **Combine** | Build a weighted view from two to six measures. |
| **Coverage** | Explore modelled outdoor radio coverage. |
| **Locations** | Screen areas for potential infrastructure. |
| **Activity** | Review institutional and civic activity. |

**Ranking** colours the map by the dimension selected in **Rank municipalities by**: **Deployment**, **Demand**, **Infrastructure**, **Institutions** or **Data**. The information icon next to the dimension explains its inputs and **How the score is calculated**. Scores run from 0 to 100 and are relative to all municipalities in the release; they are not percentages. A municipality without enough data shows **Insufficient evidence**.

**Evidence** maps a single source measure; the information icon next to each measure describes it. **Combine** builds a view under **Layer composition** from two to six measures: for each layer, choose the measure, whether **Higher** or **Lower** values count in favour, and its weight. **Add layer** adds a measure until **Maximum of 6 layers** is reached.

**Coverage** shows modelled outdoor radio coverage. **Nationwide comparison** switches between **Combined**, **Coverage** and **Redundancy**, and **Coverage summary scope** summarises the results by **Municipality**, **County** or **Voivodeship**. With **Selected station footprint** on, selecting a site on the map overlays its modelled footprint.

## General view

The general view is for finding places and inspecting the map layers:

* **Find a municipality** — type at least two characters and select a result; the map zooms to the municipality and opens its details;
* **Map layers** — show or hide **Municipalities and indices** and **Site locations**;
* **Timeline** — the data snapshot in use, shown as **Data as of** followed by the release date;
* **Site filters** — limit the sites shown to one operator or one technology.

## Details of a municipality or site

Selecting a municipality opens its details on the right: **Indices** with **Data coverage and quality**, **Details**, **Source data** and, when available, **Investment and procurement signals**. Selecting a site selects it on the map; **Open station characteristics** then shows **Station details**, such as the operator and technologies and, where available, a modelled coverage footprint. When the details are closed, **Open municipality details** reopens them.

## How to read the results

* Results are relative research indices within one data release. They are not a map of an operator's actual coverage, a signal quality measurement or a forecast of an investment decision.
* There is no single overall ranking of municipalities; each dimension answers a different question.
* Missing data does not mean zero, no infrastructure or municipal reluctance.
* Layer transparency reflects the quality and coverage of the underlying evidence.
* Modelled coverage is a planning tool, not a field measurement or a guarantee of service at an address.

Data reflects what was available on the stated date. Later updates are published as separate releases, so earlier results remain comparable.
