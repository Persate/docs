---
title: Creating an account
description: Registration via email and password, or single sign-on with Google or Microsoft.
---

Account registration is available through two methods. To begin, navigate to [persate.com](https://persate.com) and select **Sign up**.

## Method A — email and password

The registration form requires the following information:

- **Email address.** A verification link is sent to this address; it must therefore be accessible at the time of registration.
- **Password.** A minimum of eight characters, including at least one digit and at least one special character (such as `!`, `@`, `#`, or `?`). The form indicates in real time which requirements have not yet been satisfied.
- **Password confirmation.** The same password, entered a second time.
- **Acknowledgements.** The Privacy Policy and Terms of Service checkboxes must both be selected.

Once the form is submitted, the application displays a confirmation that, if verification is required, a link will arrive shortly, with a reminder to sign in or reset the password if an account already exists. The verification email contains a link that, when opened, completes the registration.

<Callout title="Verification email not received">
The message may have been delivered to a spam folder. If it is still absent after several minutes, the address has likely been mistyped — the registration form deliberately does not disclose whether an address is already in use, in order to protect existing accounts.
</Callout>

## Method B — single sign-on (Google or Microsoft)

In place of completing the registration form, select **Continue using Google Account** or **Continue using Microsoft Account**. After choosing the relevant account in the provider's dialogue and approving the requested permissions, the registration is complete.

Single sign-on combines registration and sign-in into a single action; no separate "sign up" step is required for subsequent visits. An account is created automatically on first use.

## Subsequent step

Once the email address has been verified (or, in the case of single sign-on, once the provider's dialogue has been confirmed), the account is active. The next sign-in will prompt for two-factor authentication setup.
