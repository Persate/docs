---
title: Account creation
description: Registration methods on the platform — traditional form and single sign-on (SSO).
---

Initiate registration on the [persate.com](https://persate.com) homepage by selecting **Sign up**. Two registration paths are available.

## Email and password form

Traditional registration requires completing the following fields:

* **Email address** — where the verification link message is sent.
* **Password** — a minimum of eight characters, including one digit and one special character.
* **Password confirmation** — to ensure it is entered without typos.
* **Acknowledgements** — acceptance of the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Once the form is submitted, a message is delivered to the provided address. Open the link contained within it to activate the account.

**Note:** The verification message may be delivered to the spam folder. If the link does not arrive within a few minutes, verify the correctness of the entered address.

## Single sign-on (SSO)

An account can also be created by selecting **Continue using Google Account** or **Continue using Microsoft Account**. After signing in, the account is created automatically. This method bypasses the email address verification step.

## Empty view and error states

* *„Password too weak”* — the entered password does not meet security requirements.
* *„Passwords do not match”* — the values in the password and confirmation fields differ.
* *„Email already taken”* — the platform does not allow creating a second account with the same address.
* *„Invalid email format”* — the entered text is not a valid email address.
