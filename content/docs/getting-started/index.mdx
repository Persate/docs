Persate is used within an organization. An account opens the platform only once it belongs to an organization; the organization's administrators add members, choose their role and assign them to groups.

## Ways to get access

* **Invitation from an administrator** — the usual route. The invitation email leads to a page where the password is set. See [Account creation](/getting-started/create-account).
* **Invitation for an existing account** — an account that already exists but belongs to no organization receives a link to join the inviting organization.
* **Account set up by an administrator** — an administrator can also create the account with a password; no invitation email is sent then, and the sign-in details come from the administrator.
* **Book a demo** — an organization that does not use Persate yet can select **Book a demo** on persate.com. The request form asks for a name, a work email, the organization, a role and a short description of the goal; a member of the Persate team then replies by email to arrange a time. Sending the form does not book a meeting.

**Sign up** under the sign-in form creates an account, but Persate does not open the platform for it until an administrator adds it to an organization: signing in is refused or ends on **Access Denied** with the message *Your account is not assigned to a Persate organization.*

## Before you start

* Access to the mailbox of the account's email address.
* A phone or another device with an authenticator app that generates six-digit codes. Two-step verification is required for every Persate account.

## Steps

1. **[Account creation](/getting-started/create-account)** — accept the invitation and set a password, or sign up.
2. **[Two-factor authentication](/getting-started/set-up-two-factor)** — pair the authenticator app during the first sign-in.
3. **[First sign-in](/getting-started/first-login)** — sign in and open the Cockpit.

[Using Persate](/using-persate) then explains navigation, search and notifications, and [Cockpit](/cockpit) the start screen.

## Common problems

* **This sign-in link expired** or **We couldn't sign you in** — links in Persate emails work only once and expire after a short time. Ask the administrator for a new invitation, or request a new password reset as described in [First sign-in](/getting-started/first-login). When several emails arrive, use only the newest link.
* **Access Denied** after signing in — the account is not assigned to an organization. Open the invitation link from the email, or ask an organization administrator for an invitation.
* **Request received** after signing up, but no email arrives — check the address and the spam folder. The message is the same whether or not the address already has an account.
