---
title: Users and groups
description: Inviting members, changing roles, removing members, and managing groups and their file access in Settings.
---

Organization administrators manage members and groups in **Settings**, under **ORGANIZATION → Members** and **ORGANIZATION → Groups & permissions**. Both pages are shown only to administrators. Older administration links open the same pages.

## Members

**Members** lists the active members of the organization. Above the table:

* the search field (**Name or email…**) finds members by name or email;
* **Filters** narrows the list by **Role**, **Group** and **Last active** (**Any time**, **Last 7 days**, **Last 30 days** or **Not available**); the button shows how many filters are active;
* **Invite member** opens the invitation dialog.

The table shows each **Member** with their email, **Role**, **Groups**, **Tokens this cycle**, **Last active** and **Joined**. The administrator's own row is marked **You**. When the window is too narrow for every column, the missing values are listed under the member's name. Selecting a name or a row opens the member's profile.

**Last active** is the last use of a session that passed two-step verification since the member joined the organization. **Not available** means no timestamp is recorded; it does not mean that the member has never used Persate. Sessions without two-step verification are not counted, so members who have not set it up show **Not available**.

## Invite a member

1. Select **Invite member**. The **Add user** dialog opens.
2. Enter the **Email** and choose the **Role**: **Admin** or **Member**.
3. Select **Send invite**. Persate emails an invitation with a link for setting a password.

If an account with that address already exists but does not belong to any organization, the dialog asks whether to send it an invitation to join; **Send invite** confirms.

**Set a password manually (skip the invite email)** creates the account at once, without an invitation email. The password needs at least 12 characters, and the button changes to **Create user**. Use this option only when the organization's approved identity procedure requires it; the administrator is then responsible for delivering the initial password securely.

## Invitations

Pending and expired invitations appear in a separate **Invites** table below the members, with **Email**, **Role** and **Status** (**Pending** or **Expired**). Search and filters apply to both tables, and each table has its own pages.

* **Resend invite** sends the invitation again.
* **Revoke invite** cancels the invitation.

## Change a role

The **Role** control in a member's row, or on the member's profile, switches between **Admin** and **Member**; the change is saved at once. An administrator cannot change their own role or remove themselves from the organization — in their own row these controls are inactive, and another administrator has to make the change. The organization always keeps at least one active administrator: a change that would leave none is refused with **The organization must keep at least one active administrator.** If a page still shows the old role after a change, refresh it or sign in again.

## Member profile

The profile shows the member's usage for the selected period — **7d**, **30d**, **90d** or **Billing cycle** — including events, files, tokens and last activity, the most used features and token trends. Activity is limited to event metadata.

The side panel shows the member's **Role**, **Status**, **Last active**, **Joined**, **Private storage** and **Groups**. **Add to group** adds the member to another group with the group role **Member**; selecting a group opens it.

## Remove a member

Select the bin icon in the member's row, or **Remove from organization** on the profile, and confirm. Removal deletes the member's account and private files and cannot be undone. Before removing a member, transfer or export work that must be kept and review the groups and files the member shares.

## Groups

**Groups & permissions** lists the organization's groups with their description, **Members**, **Managers**, **Created** date and the number of shared **Files**. The search field finds groups by name or description, and **Filters** limits the list to groups with a given **Manager**.

The organization-wide group, **Everyone at** followed by the organization's name, includes every member. It is managed by the organization's administrators: its membership is fixed, and it cannot be edited or deleted. Files can still be shared with it.

### Create a group

1. Select **Create group**. The **Create New Group** dialog opens.
2. Enter the **Name** and, optionally, the **Description**.
3. Select **Create Group**.

### Manage a group

Selecting a group, or its pencil icon (**Manage Group**), opens its page with the **Members** and **Files** tabs. **Edit** changes the name and description, and **Delete Group** deletes the group.

**Members** tab:

* **Add member** opens **Add member to group**: choose the **User** and the **Role** in the group, then select **Add to group**.
* Each row shows the **Group role**, the **Organization role**, **Last active** and the **Added** date. A changed **Group role** is saved at once.
* The bin icon removes the member from the group.

Group roles are **Member**, **Manager** and **Viewer**. Members and managers can share content with the group; a viewer sees what is shared with the group but cannot share into it — in sharing controls the group is marked **Read only**.

**Files** tab:

* **Share file** opens a picker for choosing a Repository file to share with the group.
* **Remove File Access** stops sharing the file with the group. The file itself is not deleted.

### Delete a group

A group can be deleted only after its files are no longer shared with it: remove its file access first, then select **Delete Group** and confirm. Deleting a group cannot be undone and does not delete the files.

## Access troubleshooting

* A member cannot see a shared file — check the member's group membership, the file's sharing with that group and that Repository is enabled for the organization.
* A member cannot see a lab — labs are assigned to groups; check the member's groups, then contact the organization's administrator or Persate about the assignment.
* A member still sees an old role — the member should refresh the page or sign in again.
* An action failed — reload the list before trying again, so that the action applies to the current state.
