---
title: Published acts and publications
description: Browse official journals, read acts in the structured reader, and follow the publications of ministries and offices.
---

The **Published acts** tab opens the sources of published law and official publications. Sources appear as tiles in two groups:

* **Official journals** — national journals, voivodeship journals, and other official sources of legal acts. A tile shows the type of source (**National journal**, **Voivodeship journal**, or **Official source**), the number of acts, and the time of the latest update.
* **Ministries and offices** — publications of ministries, the Chancellery of the Prime Minister, and other public institutions, when included in the organization's plan. The group lists the institutions currently available; more institutions are added gradually.

**Search sources** in the sidebar filters both groups by name. **No matching sources** means no source matches the name; try a different one. If the institutions cannot be loaded, **Couldn't load ministries and offices.** is shown with **Try again**.

## Browsing a journal

Selecting a journal tile opens its page: a banner with the source name and the number of acts, and a table with **Title**, **Type**, **Status**, and **Published**. Further rows load while scrolling.

The sidebar filters the acts of that source:

* **Search this source** — text search within the journal.
* **Document type** — **Act**, **Regulation**, **Notice**, or **International agreement**.
* **Status** — **In force**, **Consolidated**, **Repealed**, or **Pending**.
* **Published** — the publication date range.

**Reset filters** clears all four. Selecting a row opens a preview panel with the original file, when one is attached, and the publication details. The panel offers **Open full view** and, when an original file is attached, **Download original PDF**.

## Reading an act

The full view of an act shows its title, status, and publication identifier (ELI). The side panel contains:

* **Actions** — when an original file is attached, a switch between **Reader**, the structured text, and **Original**, the original file, plus **Download original**; **View legislative journey** opens the act's legislative journey.
* **Details** — **ELI**, **Type**, **Status**, **Published**, **Last change**, **Print №**, **Publisher**, and **Language**, when available.
* **Contents** — the sections of the act; select a section to jump to it.

The reader presents the text unit by unit. Units that are no longer in force are marked **Repealed**. If the text is not ready, the reader states why: **Full text not available yet** (only the metadata is available; check the original if one is attached), **No readable text**, or **Couldn't load the text**.

When the act is opened from an Advisor citation, the reader scrolls to the cited passage and highlights it.

## Ministries and offices

Selecting an institution tile opens its publications: a banner with the institution name and the number of publications, and a table with **Title**, **Category**, and **Published**. Rows for attached files carry a file icon, and a short excerpt or the site section under the title gives context.

The sidebar filters the publications:

* **Search this institution** — text search within the institution's publications.
* **Category** — select one or more categories: **News**, **Announcement**, **Press release**, **Consultation**, **Draft act**, **Official journal**, **Petition**, **Report**, **Grant competition**, or **Other**. With none selected, all default categories are shown.
* **Show all categories** — recruitment and procurement notices are hidden by default; this switch adds **Recruitment** and **Procurement**.
* **Published** — the publication date range.

**Reset filters** restores the defaults. The filters are kept in the page address, so returning from a publication restores the same filtered list, and a filtered list can be shared as a link.

### Reading a publication

A publication page shows its category, the institution, the publication date, and **Open the source page**, which opens the original page on the institution's site. The text appears in the same reader as acts. Publications with files list them under **Attachments**; select a file (**Read the text**) to read it in the reader, and use **Back to the page text** to return. The side panel lists **Institution**, **Category**, **Published**, **Section**, and **Type** (**Web page** or **File**). Source content retains its original language.

Publications of an institution also appear under **Latest publications** on its profile in [Stakeholders](/stakeholders), and in the **Ministries and offices** category of search. In the Tracker, an alert's source list can include **Ministries and offices — publications**.

### Empty and error states

* **No matching publications** — no publication matches the filters; change the search or the filters.
* **No publications yet** — there is nothing to show yet; new publications appear here once the institution publishes them.
* **Couldn't load publications.** — select **Try again**.
* **Institution not found** — the institution's publications are not monitored; pick another one from the list.
* **Publication not found** — the link no longer resolves.
* **This feature is not included in your plan** — the organization's plan does not include institution publications; contact an administrator.
