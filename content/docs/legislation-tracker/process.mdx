title: Process and legislative journeys
description: Find government legislative projects, filter them by stage and attributes, and follow a bill from drafting to publication.
---

The **Process** tab lists government legislative projects (RCL) and opens the legislative journey of each one. A journey brings the whole process together on one page: the government draft, the Sejm, the Senate, the President, a veto or Constitutional Tribunal referral when one occurs, and publication.

## Project list

The table shows **Title**, **Type**, **Status**, **Applicant**, and **Updated**; on narrow screens some columns are hidden and the identifier and date appear under the title. Select a row to open the project's journey.

The status says where the project's process stands. When the project has a Sejm print, the print's record decides it; otherwise the government register does. The register rarely closes a project once it goes to the Sejm, so a project the register still lists as open can read **Withdrawn** or **Enacted**. Hover over a status to see what it rests on and the date of the last RCL document.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Government drafting** | The draft is open in RCL and was started or carried on by the current government. |
| **Referred to the Sejm** | RCL records the referral to the Sejm, but no Sejm print is linked yet. |
| **In the Sejm**, **In the Senate**, **With the President**, **At the Tribunal** | The bill is at that stage. |
| **Vetoed** | The President vetoed the act; it becomes law only if the Sejm overrides the veto. |
| **Enacted** | The act was signed or published in the Journal of Laws. |
| **Adopted** | Closed in RCL: a regulation signed and published, or a bill or assumptions adopted by the government. |
| **Not continued** | RCL still lists the draft as open, but it was started under an earlier government, and nothing has been added to it since the current government took office on 13 December 2023. |
| **Withdrawn**, **Rejected**, **Lapsed**, **Veto upheld** | The process ended without the act becoming law. |

A government draft ends only through a formal act: the Council of Ministers adopts or rejects it, a minister signs it, the ministry withdraws it in RCL, or the government's work register records that work on it was abandoned. No rule ends a draft because time has passed or the government has changed, so a draft that nobody carried on reads **Not continued** rather than **Withdrawn**.

## Filters

* **Current stage** — the institution where a process still in progress currently is: **Government drafting**, **Sejm**, **Senate**, **President**, or **Constitutional Tribunal**. A withdrawn, lapsed, or enacted project has no current stage. Several stages can be selected.
* **Search projects** — text search across projects.
* **Project type** — for example **Act** or **Regulation**, with the number of projects of each type.
* **Applicant** — the authority that submitted the project, with the number of its projects.
* **Created** — the creation date range.
* **Work register no.** — the number in the government's work register, for example `RD456`.
* **Status** — one or more of the statuses above.
* **Attributes** — **Implements EU law**, **Executes Constitutional Tribunal ruling**, **Based on draft-act assumptions**, **Separate legislative mode**, **Announced in the Journal of Laws**, and **Referred to the Sejm**. Selecting attributes narrows the list to projects that have them.

The **Short titles** and **Pin tracked projects** switches and the **Clear filters** action are described in [Legislation](/legislation-tracker). If no project matches, **No projects found** is shown; change the search or the filters. **Couldn't load projects** means the list could not be retrieved; reload the page.

## Legislative journey

A journey opens from the project list, from Advisor chips, from search results, and from records in [Votes](/voting-ledger). The breadcrumb leads back to **Projects**.

### Status

The badge above the title states where the process stands:

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **In progress** | The process is ongoing. |
| **Enacted** | The bill became law. |
| **Vetoed** | The President vetoed the act and the Sejm has not yet voted on the veto. |
| **Veto overridden** | The Sejm passed the act again after the veto. |
| **Veto upheld** | The Sejm did not override the veto; the process is closed. |
| **At the Constitutional Tribunal** | The act has been referred to the Constitutional Tribunal. |
| **Rejected** | The bill was rejected. |
| **Withdrawn** | The proposer withdrew the bill. |
| **Lapsed at the end of the term** | The process was discontinued when the parliamentary term ended. |
| **Abandoned** | The process closed without the bill becoming law, for another reason. |
| **Status unknown** | The available records do not establish the status. |

For a government project without a Sejm print, the badge shows the project's own status from the list above, for example **Government drafting**, **Adopted**, or **Not continued**.

### Summary

When available, an automated summary opens the page, organized under its own headings. Document chips in the summary open the cited project document in a preview panel. The summary is an aid to reading; the documents remain authoritative.

### Process Timeline

**Process Timeline** groups the stages into phases in the order the process entered them: **Draft register**, **Sejm**, **Senate**, **Sejm review of the Senate position**, **President**, **Veto reconsideration**, **Constitutional Tribunal**, **Constitutional remediation**, and the publication journal, for example **Journal of Laws**. A process that returns to an institution, for example after a veto, shows that return as a new phase. Each phase shows its number of stages and the latest date. The latest phase opens by default; select a phase or a stage to expand it. Stages that have not happened yet are shown but cannot be opened.

An expanded stage can show:

* the committee the bill was referred to;
* official decisions, for example **Senate proposed amendments**, **Signed by the President**, **Vetoed by the President**, **Veto overridden — act passed again**, or **Consistent with the Constitution**, each with its date and an **Official source** link;
* the result of a vote held at that stage: votes for, against, and abstaining;
* the stage documents: government project documents open in the preview panel, and Sejm prints open their own legislative journey or the official file;
* for a published act, its name and publication identifier.

Votes that cannot be assigned to a stage are listed under **Other votes**. If the opened print is part of another print's process, a note says so and the page shows the full journey of that process.

### Document preview

A government project document opens in a panel on the right. A PDF is shown in full. For other formats, **Extracted text** shows the text when it is available; long documents show their first part. If neither is available, **Preview not available** is shown. The side of the panel shows the document's **Summary** when one exists, its **Information**, and **Download original file**.

### Change log

For government projects, **Change log** lists the official register events and Persate's automated change analyses in a table with **Date**, **Author**, and **Event**. Analyses carry the author **Persate**; they can be marked **Material change** and name the affected groups after **Affected:**. **Load more** shows older entries. **No changes recorded** means no events or analyses are available yet.

### Side panel

* **Actions** — **Track this project** (or **See alert** once tracked), **Open in RCL**, and, for a published act, the link to the official publication.
* **Details** — the status, the legal force of a published act (for example **In force** or **Repealed**), the print and bill numbers, the publication identifier, the RCL identifier, the work register entry, type, submission type, applicant, key dates including the last RCL document, and flags such as **EU-related** or **Public consultation**. Only values present in the records are shown.
* **Legal basis** and **Comment** — the legal basis and the register comment of a government project, when published.

**Legislative journey not found** means the link no longer resolves; return to the project list. **No process stages** means no timeline events are available for the project yet.
