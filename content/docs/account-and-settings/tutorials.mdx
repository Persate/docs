## Choose a tutorial

Open **Settings → Interactive tutorials**. The library is grouped like the navigation, with **Settings** last, and lists only the modules available to the account. The **Getting started** group holds the tutorials for the whole platform and the **Guides** to features available everywhere, such as the navigation and Search. Some organization guides are listed only for organization administrators.

The tutorials are:

* **Getting started** — opens a story in the Cockpit and its source, finds the Electronic Communications Law in Legislation, follows it into the Sejm and drafts a question for the Advisor. It is offered when the Cockpit, Legislation and Advisor are all enabled.
* **Find anything with Search** — searches for the Electronic Communications Law, narrows the results to **Legislation**, previews the law and opens its page.
* **Find a bill, read its process** — in Legislation, finds the Electronic Communications Law, compares statuses with the **Status** filter, expands the government's work and its public consultation, opens one of the positions submitted in the consultation, follows the bill into the Sejm and opens it in RCL.
* **Check what an alert found** — in Tracker, opens the first enabled alert, expands its newest match and checks its source and matching sensitivity.
* **Replay a moment from a sitting** — in Recordings, opens the Education and Science Committee sitting of 16 June 2026, jumps to a moment from the transcript, checks who is speaking and plays a statement from the official transcript.
* **Prepare a question** — in Advisor, drafts a question about the Electronic Communications Law, chooses the analysis depth and attaches the law from context search, without sending anything.

Where the data is public, the tutorials use the same examples for every account: the Electronic Communications Law (*Prawo komunikacji elektronicznej*), whose 2024 draft was enacted and published as Dz.U. 2024 poz. 1221, and a committee sitting with a published official transcript. Cockpit stories and alerts belong to the account, so those steps use the first one on the screen and are skipped when there is none.

Each tutorial shows a progress bar and its status, such as **Completed**. Selecting its name expands the steps: completed steps are ticked, and **Next step** marks where the tutorial continues. The button beside it reads **Start tutorial**, **Resume tutorial** or **Repeat tutorial**; an interrupted tutorial also offers **Start over**. A module's guides are collected in one row, such as **Guides: Tracker**. Expand it and select **Open** beside a guide; a guide that explains a particular page opens that page too.

The **Getting started** section at the bottom of the sidebar offers the same entry points:

* **Interactive tutorial** starts the Getting started tutorial, or resumes it from the saved step, with a counter showing progress. On the first visit, a tooltip beside it offers a short tour; closing the tooltip dismisses the offer, and the entry remains. Once the tutorial is completed, or when it is not available, **Interactive tutorials** takes its place and opens the library.
* When the current page has its own unfinished tutorial, that tutorial appears here by name.
* **Guide & FAQ** opens the guide for the current page, or the library when the page has no guide.
* **Documentation** opens this documentation in a new tab.

On a phone, **Guide & FAQ** and **Interactive tutorials** are in the navigation menu of the dock at the bottom of the screen.

Save unfinished work before starting a tutorial: its first step may open another page.

## Follow the steps

The tutorial points at one control at a time: a ring marks the control, and a numbered tooltip beside it says what to do. The application stays fully usable, and the tutorial does not click, type or send anything on the user's behalf. When the action is detected, the ring confirms it with a tick and moves to the next control, on another page if needed. While a page opens or its content loads, the tooltip asks you to wait a moment; if the control is still not there once the page has settled, it says so and suggests scrolling or skipping the step. A click on another control of the same kind, such as another phase, document or tab, is pointed out, and the tooltip repeats what to click. If a window covers the control, the pointer shows how to close it.

Steps that the page has nothing for, such as opening a story on an empty Cockpit, are skipped automatically. When a step asks for typing, Enter does not send the text, so a practice question does not reach the Advisor. After leaving the page, view or panel that a step needs, the tutorial pauses instead of sending you back, and so does the narration. The tooltip reads **The tutorial is paused** and points the way back: at the control that reopens the view or panel, or at the module in the navigation. From another page, **Back to the tutorial** opens the same place again and continues the same step, without starting over or repeating it; **Stop tutorial** saves the step reached. Returning by yourself also continues the same step. If a step's control needs an earlier step again, such as a stage that was collapsed, the pointer shows that control first. On a phone, the pointer may first mark the navigation menu button.

While a tutorial runs, the **Getting started** section lists its steps with a progress bar: completed steps are ticked and the current step is highlighted. With the sidebar collapsed to icons, only the step number is shown.

## Guide and FAQ

Every module has guides that explain its pages. In the browser, the question-mark button at the left of the header opens the guide for the current page; its tooltip reads **Guide & FAQ**. When something with its own guide is open, such as a recording, that guide opens instead. Selecting the button again closes the guide. On a page without a guide, the button opens this documentation; in the desktop app, it always does.

The guide opens in a panel on the right, and the page stays usable beside it. Esc or a click outside the panel closes it. The panel has two tabs:

* **What to click** — the page's controls, numbered, each with what it does. **Show me** closes the panel and marks the control on the page with its name and explanation; the **Guide** button at the right edge of the screen brings the panel back. A control that is not on the screen is greyed out. If the guide's page is not open, **Show me** opens it first.
* **FAQ** — answers to common questions about the page.

**More guides** lists the module's other guides. The footer of the panel starts the page's tutorial, if it has one, and links to the **Documentation**. When another page opens, the panel switches to that page's guide.

## Sound controls

Tutorials are narrated in the interface language. The control strip below the steps holds:

* a line that follows the narrator's voice while it speaks and stays flat in silence;
* the speaker button, **Mute tutorial audio** or **Unmute tutorial audio**, and a volume slider that also responds to the arrow keys;
* **Skip step** (the arrow), which moves on to the next step;
* **Stop tutorial** (the cross).

Sound is off on the first visit. Moving the slider to zero mutes the narration, and moving it up turns the sound back on. Muting or changing the volume does not restart the current instruction. The browser remembers the sound setting and the volume. While the narrator speaks, the tooltip shows a small sound wave and **Pause narration**, which stops the voice where it is; **Resume narration** continues it, and the next step is read as usual. The sound controls appear only when narration is available in the interface language.

## Pause and return

**Stop tutorial** ends the tutorial and saves the step reached. Starting it again from the sidebar, from the guide panel or with **Resume tutorial** in the library continues from that step; **Start over** begins from the first step. Completing the last step records the tutorial as completed, and the library then offers **Repeat tutorial**.

Progress is saved to the account, so it is available on other devices. If saving fails, **Not saved. Retry** appears below the controls; in the library, **Try again** retries loading or saving progress.

Tutorials, guides and narration follow the interface language. With reduced motion enabled on the device, the pointer moves without animation and the voice line stays still.
