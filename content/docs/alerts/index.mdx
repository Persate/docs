**Alerts and risks** are used for continuous, automated monitoring of incoming signals, broadcasts, files, and social media activity for defined topics and keywords. When a match is found, a **trigger event** is recorded.

Access to the module is provided by the **INTELLIGENCE → Alerts & Risks** entry in the sidebar.

## Monitoring channels

The platform monitors four types of content:

* **Sejm speech** — speech transcripts from Sejm recordings.
* **Public pulse** — posts from the X accounts of monitored stakeholders.
* **File** — documents uploaded to the repository.
* **Live** — fragments of broadcasted sessions captured in real time.

## Alert list view

The main view presents a grid of cards for all configured alerts.

### Alert card

Every card contains:

* **Banner** — a thematic illustration matched based on the alert's topic.
* **Status** — **ACTIVE** or **PAUSED**.
* **Name and description** — labels identifying the monitored topic.
* **Last trigger** — the date of the most recently recorded match.

Selecting a card opens the detailed view with the history of all events.

## Sidebar

Management functions are available in the right sidebar:

* **Create alert** — opens the form to create a new alert.
* **Search** — enables filtering the alert list by name or description.

## Alert lifecycle

An alert can be in one of three states:

* **Active** — the platform actively searches for matches in new data.
* **Paused** — new matches are not executed, but the historical record remains available.
* **Deleted** — the alert, along with its entire history, is permanently and irreversibly removed.

## Empty view and error states

* *„No alerts found”* — no alerts have been created on the account yet, or search filters exclude all entries.
* *„Couldn't load alerts”* — server communication error. Refreshing the page is recommended.
