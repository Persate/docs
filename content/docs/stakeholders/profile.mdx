title: Stakeholder profile
description: A person's profile with sourced profile sections, details, available data, voting activity, campaign promises and financial disclosures, and tracking the person with an alert.
---

A person's profile opens from a person tile in the **Organizations** search, a row in a members table, a node in a [Connections](/stakeholders/connections) graph or a citation in an Advisor answer.

## Header

The header shows the photo, the name and a line with the current role and the party group or institution (*Public profile* when no role is known). For party members, the banner uses the party's colors and logo.

## Profile sections

The main column presents the profile as thematic sections, such as **Summary**, **Biography**, **Education**, **Professional career**, **Political career**, **Current mandate**, **Political positions**, **Voting record**, **Public statements**, **Affiliations & interests** and **Finances**. Only sections with content appear. On a wide screen, a table of contents on the left follows the reading position; on a phone, the section list (**Contents**) is at the bottom of the **Details** panel.

Under a section, **Sources:** lists what it was built from, with links when available. **Show raw sources** expands the original source fragments. Each fragment carries a label naming its origin (for example official parliamentary records, public reference databases or editorial notes), a link to the original and an **Edited** badge when it was corrected by hand.

When generated sections are not available, the profile shows the plain biography or *Profile not analyzed yet*.

## Campaign promises and financial disclosures

For members of the Sejm, two more sections can appear:

* **Campaign Promises** — promises with tags and links to their sources.
* **Financial Disclosures** — asset declarations. The buttons with filing dates switch between declarations; each declaration shows its filled-in parts: **Cash & savings**, **Real estate**, **Income**, **Movable assets**, **Liabilities** and **Shares & stocks**.

## Details panel

The right-hand panel (on a phone, the **Details** button) contains:

* **Actions** — **Track person**, or **See alert** once an alert for the person exists.
* **Details** — **Role**, **Institution**, **Profession**, **Education**, **Date of birth**, **Place of birth**, **Voivodeship**, **District**, **Votes received**, **Email** and **Source**, a link to the official profile the data comes from. Only known values appear; otherwise the group shows *No additional profile details are available.*
* **Available data** — tiles for the data Persate holds about the person: **Face** and **Voice** (the person can be recognized by face or voice in analyzed recordings) and **X** (Persate follows the person's posts on X).
* **Online presence** — links to the person's social media profiles, website and public reference pages.
* **Voting activity** — the **Attendance** bar, **Votes cast** (out of all votings) and **Absences**. The group appears only when voting statistics are available.

## Track a person

1. Under **Actions**, select **Track person**.
2. In the **Alert creation** dialog, set **Access** — **Only me** or selected groups — and choose the **Sensitivity**.
3. Select **Start tracking**.

When an existing alert can take the person as a further case, the dialog also offers **Tracking destination**: choose **Add this case to an existing alert**, pick the alert and select **Add to alert**. The existing alert keeps its audience and settings.

The alert follows substantive public and legislative mentions of this exact person, such as speeches, votes, authored documents and media. Once it exists, **See alert** opens it in [Tracker](/alerts).

## Empty and error states

* *Not found* — the address does not match any person in Persate. Return to the **Organizations** tab and search again.
* *Profile not analyzed yet* — generated profile sections appear after the person has been analyzed.
* *No additional profile details are available.* — no details are known beyond the name and role.
