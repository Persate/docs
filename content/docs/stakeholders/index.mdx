**Stakeholders** brings together the institutions, parliamentary groups and people that matter in Polish public affairs. Open it from **PUBLIC → Stakeholders** in the sidebar. The module is available when it is enabled for the organization.

## Tabs

Two tabs stay at the top of every page in the module:

* **Organizations** — a grid of institutions and parties with a search across organizations and people. Organization pages, the Sejm page and person profiles open under this tab.
* **Connections** — research that maps how people, organizations and legal acts relate to a topic. See [Connections](/stakeholders/connections).

## Browse organizations

The **Organizations** tab opens with a grid of tiles: the Sejm and the Senate, the President of Poland, the government (the Council of Ministers, the Prime Minister's Office and the ministries) and the parliamentary parties and groups. Each tile shows the number of members or staff.

Selecting a tile opens the organization page. The **Sejm** tile opens a dedicated page with committees and members, described in [Sejm and committees](/stakeholders/sejm).

## Search for an organization or a person

Type in the **Search** field in the right-hand panel. The grid narrows with each character and the remaining tiles grow to fill the space. From the second character onwards, the search also:

* shows tiles for matching people at the start of the grid; selecting one opens the person's profile;
* keeps organizations whose members match the query, so a politician's surname also brings up their party or institution;
* adds matching institutions that are not part of the default grid.

When nothing matches, *No results* appears with a hint to try another organization name or politician surname.

## Organization pages

An organization page opens with a banner that shows the name and, for a subordinate unit, the organization it belongs to (*Part of: …*). Below the banner:

* **Latest publications** — the institution's newest publications with their category and date. Selecting one opens it; **See all** opens the institution's full list in [Legislation](/legislation-tracker). The list appears only when the institution has publications in Persate and this part of Legislation is enabled for the organization.
* The members table — **Name**, **Role**, **Party Group** and **Voivodeship**. On a phone the table shows the name and role. Further members load while scrolling; selecting a row opens the person's profile.

The right-hand panel (on a phone, the **Filters** button) contains:

* **Details** — **Active members** and, when known, **Part of** and **Short name**.
* **Available data** — tiles for the data Persate holds about the organization: **X** (Persate follows the organization's posts on X) and **Publications** (Persate collects its publications).
* **Search people** — filters the members table by name.

## Links from Advisor

When an Advisor answer cites a person or a Sejm committee, the citation opens the person's profile or the committee page.

## Empty and error states

* *No results* — no organization or person matches the search.
* *No members* — the organization has no members in the current term.
* *Could not load the organization.* — the organization page failed to load; reload the page.
