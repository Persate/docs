**Stakeholders** brings together people, institutions and political groups. Open **PUBLIC → Stakeholders** in the sidebar. The module requires access in the organization's package.

## Browse people and organizations

Use the tabs at the top — **People**, **Public institutions**, **Parliamentary clubs** and **Parliament** — to choose the list. Stakeholders opens with **People**.

The table follows the same layout as the Repository. People show their name, role, parliamentary club, electoral district and voivodeship, with email on wider screens. Public institutions show their full name, type and official website, plus an address where available. Parliamentary clubs show their full name, member count and public contact details. Portraits, logos and official emblems identify the rows. Missing source data is left blank.

The institution directory includes the Sejm, Senate, Presidential Chancellery, Constitutional Tribunal and Supreme Court alongside ministries and public agencies. Club marks use compact official variants at a consistent size. In the people table, the district and voivodeship share one line; longer values reveal their full text on hover.

Checkboxes appear when hovering over a row and remain visible after selection. The header checkbox selects the loaded rows. On touch screens the checkboxes are always visible. Selection is retained as more rows load and cleared when changing the search, filters or category. Clicking a person or institution name or row opens its profile; clicking a parliamentary club opens the filtered member list.

Selecting profiles reveals a small floating toolbar at the bottom of the screen, with a selection count and icons for **Ask the Advisor**, **Search the platform**, **Find related files** and **Clear selection**. Hover or focus an icon to see its label. The Advisor action opens a question field and includes every selected profile when you submit it. Search lets you switch between the selected people or organizations; the file action restricts retrieval to the Repository.

The **Parliament** tab opens [the Sejm and Senate](/stakeholders/sejm). Choose the chamber in the sidebar and switch **People** / **Committees** in the narrow icon rail beside it. Sejm and Senate rows in the institution directory open the corresponding chamber.

## Search and filters

The right sidebar contains **Search**, the available filters and sorting controls. On a phone, select **Filters** to open this panel. Enter at least two characters of a name. Search applies to the whole selected category.

The people list also supports the **Parliamentary club** filter. When browsing people without a search query, **Sort by** changes alphabetical order. Search results are ranked by relevance. **Clear filters** removes the query and club selection and restores **Name: A–Z** sorting. Switching categories clears the club filter.

Further profiles load automatically when scrolling near the end of the table. The count below the table indicates how many profiles have been loaded, not the total size of the directory. **Back to directory** on a person's profile restores its originating search, category and filters. These settings are also retained in the directory address.

## Organization pages

An organization page opens with a banner directly below the category tabs. It shows the name and, for a subordinate unit, the organization it belongs to (*Part of: …*). Below the banner:

* **Latest publications** — the institution's newest publications with their category and date. Selecting one opens it; **See all** opens the institution's full list in [Legislation](/legislation-tracker). The list appears only when the institution has publications in Persate and this part of Legislation is enabled for the organization.
* The members table — **Name**, **Role**, **Party Group** and **Voivodeship**. On a phone the table shows the name and role. Further members load while scrolling; selecting a row opens the person's profile.

The right-hand panel (on a phone, the **Filters** button) contains:

* **Details** — **Active members** and, when known, **Part of** and **Short name**.
* **Available data** — tiles for the data Persate holds about the organization: **X** (Persate follows the organization's posts on X) and **Publications** (Persate collects its publications).
* **Search people** — filters the members table by name.

## Links from Advisor

When an Advisor answer cites a person or a Sejm committee, the citation opens the person's profile or the committee page.

## Empty and error states

* *No matching profiles* — the selected category and filters returned no matches.
* *Could not load the stakeholder directory.* — use **Try again** to retry the request.
* *No members* — the organization has no members in the current term.
* *Could not load the organization.* — the organization page failed to load; reload the page.
