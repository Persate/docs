**Stakeholders** brings together people, institutions and political groups. Open **PUBLIC → Stakeholders** in the sidebar. The module requires access in the organization's package.

## Tabs

- **Directory** — a searchable list, opening with **People**. Profiles and organization pages open from this list.
- **Connections** — research about people, organizations and legal acts related to a topic. See [Connections](/stakeholders/connections).

## Browse people and organizations

Use **People**, **Public institutions** or **Parliamentary clubs** to choose the list. Each row shows a name and available role or affiliation. Select a person or institution row to open its profile. Selecting a parliamentary club opens the filtered member list.

**Sejm and committees** opens the dedicated [Sejm page](/stakeholders/sejm).

## Search and filters

The **Search** field appears above the list, including on a phone. Enter at least two characters of a name. Search applies to the whole selected category.

The people list also supports the **Parliamentary club** filter. When browsing people without a search query, **Sort by** changes alphabetical order. Search results are ranked by relevance. **Clear filters** removes the query and club selection. Switching categories clears the club filter.

**Load more profiles** adds the next results. The count above the list indicates how many profiles have been loaded, not the total size of the directory. **Back to directory** on a profile restores its originating search, category and filters. These settings are also retained in the directory address.

## Organization pages

An organization page opens with a banner that shows the name and, for a subordinate unit, the organization it belongs to (*Part of: …*). Below the banner:

* **Latest publications** — the institution's newest publications with their category and date. Selecting one opens it; **See all** opens the institution's full list in [Legislation](/legislation-tracker). The list appears only when the institution has publications in Persate and this part of Legislation is enabled for the organization.
* The members table — **Name**, **Role**, **Party Group** and **Voivodeship**. On a phone the table shows the name and role. Further members load while scrolling; selecting a row opens the person's profile.

The right-hand panel (on a phone, the **Filters** button) contains:

* **Details** — **Active members** and, when known, **Part of** and **Short name**.
* **Available data** — tiles for the data Persate holds about the organization: **X** (Persate follows the organization's posts on X) and **Publications** (Persate collects its publications).
* **Search people** — filters the members table by name.

## Links from Advisor

When an Advisor answer cites a person or a Sejm committee, the citation opens the person's profile or the committee page.

## Empty and error states

* *No matching profiles* — the selected category and filters returned no matches.
* *Could not load the stakeholder directory.* — use **Try again** to retry the request.
* *No members* — the organization has no members in the current term.
* *Could not load the organization.* — the organization page failed to load; reload the page.
