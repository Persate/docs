**Stakeholders** is a catalog of entities relevant to public affairs — individuals, political groups, and public institutions. Every profile combines biographical materials, roles and affiliations, and related legislative and media activity.

Open the module from **PUBLIC → Stakeholders** in the sidebar. The related activity stream on X is covered by the separate [Public Pulse](/public-pulse) section.

## Tabs

Three tabs are available in the top bar of the view:

* **People** — the main entry point to the catalog in the form of a tile grid. The tiles lead to organization pages and individual profiles.
* **Sejm** — a list of chamber members along with their committee membership and information about proceedings.
* **Connections** — a comparison tool for juxtaposing stakeholders.

## Catalog overview

The **People** tab is a tile grid — the starting point for browsing the entire catalog. The tiles cover key institutions and groups arranged thematically: legislative authorities (Sejm, Senate), the Office of the President, ministries and executive bodies (among others the Council of Ministers, the Chancellery of the Prime Minister, individual ministries), and clubs and parties. Each tile leads to the page of a given organization along with a list of its members, and the Sejm tile opens the **Sejm** tab.

## Search

A **sidebar panel containing a search field** (the **Search** field) is located on the right side of the view. The search covers organizations, parties, and individuals. After entering a query:

* non-matching tiles disappear, and the remaining ones grow to fill the grid;
* alongside the matched organizations and parties, **tiles for specific individuals** appear (politicians found by name), from which the profile can be opened directly;
* matching also works by members — entering a member's name highlights the organizations and clubs they belong to.

Selecting any tile opens the corresponding page — an individual profile or an organization page.

## Empty view and error states

* *"No results"* — no organization, party, or individual matches the query. A hint appears suggesting trying a different organization name or politician's name.
