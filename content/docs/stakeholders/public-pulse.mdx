title: Public Pulse
description: Activity of monitored stakeholders on X (Twitter) presented as a list or timeline.
---

**Public Pulse** aggregates stakeholder activity on X. Every post, reply, or repost enters a shared stream that can be filtered and analyzed on a timeline.

Access to the view is provided by the **INTELLIGENCE → Public Pulse** entry in the sidebar.

## Presentation modes

Two view modes can be switched between at the top of the screen:

* **Timeline** — a vertical timeline. When multiple clubs are selected, it switches to a comparative mode, enabling the juxtaposition of narratives from different political groups.
* **Feed** — a chronological list of post cards, optimal for a quick overview of the latest activity.

## Post card

Every post is labeled with a tag indicating its nature: **Original** (own post), **Reply** (response), **Retweet** (repost), or **Quote** (repost with commentary). Posts deleted from X but previously captured are labeled as *Archived*.

## Timeline features

In **Timeline** mode, hovering over a point on the timeline displays a post preview. The **Timeline window** selector in the sidebar allows changing the post visibility range:

* **60m** — the last hour of activity.
* **24h** — the last 24 hours.

For multi-club comparisons, the **Differences only** option is available, which hides posts with topics common to the selected groups, highlighting discrepancies in the subjects of their activity.

## Filtering and trends

Tools for narrowing the stream are available in the sidebar:

* **Search** — text search within post content.
* **Parliamentary Club** and **Stakeholders** — filtering by affiliation or specific individuals.
* **Date Range** — restricting results to a selected time period.

Below the filters, the **Trending · last 24h** panel lists the most popular hashtags and mentions. Selecting a trend automatically populates the search field.

## Synchronization status

The indicator in the sidebar footer provides information about the displayed data state:

* **Blue dot** — data is up to date.
* **Amber dot** — new posts are being processed.

The text *„Last synced..."* shows the time of the last successful update.

## Recommended workflow

1. Open the view in **Feed** mode to review the latest posts.
2. Apply **Club** or **Stakeholder** filters to narrow the results.
3. Switch to **Timeline** mode (range **24h**) to perform a comparative analysis.
4. Use the **Trending** section to identify popular topics.

## Empty view and error states

* *„No tweets match your filters"* — active filters exclude all posts.
* *„No tweets yet"* — no new posts in the monitored stream.
* *„Couldn't load Public Pulse"* — server connection error. The **Retry** button allows reattempting the request.
