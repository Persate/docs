Open **PUBLIC → Stakeholders** and select the **Sejm** tile on the **Organizations** tab. The Sejm page lists the committees of the Sejm and its members.

## Find a committee

The **Committees** section is a table with **Name**, **Code**, **Type**, **Members** and **Sittings**. A committee's type is **Standing**, **Extraordinary** or **Investigative**.

Narrow the list in the right-hand panel (on a phone, the **Filters** button):

* **Committees** — search committees.
* **Committee type** — limit the table to one type; **All types** shows every committee.
* **Clear committee filters** — appears when a filter is active and resets both.

On a phone, the arrow next to the **Committees** heading folds the list so the members are quicker to reach.

## Find a member

The **Members** section lists the members of the Sejm with **Name**, **Role**, **Party Group** and **Voivodeship**. The **Search people** field in the right-hand panel filters the list by name. Further members load while scrolling; selecting a row opens the person's [profile](/stakeholders/profile).

## Committee page

Selecting a committee opens its page. The header shows the committee type, the name and the committee's scope. The page has two sections:

* **Members** — the committee's members with their committee role, party group and voivodeship. Selecting a member opens their profile.
* **Sittings** — a timeline of the committee's sittings. A planned sitting carries the **Scheduled** badge. Expanding a sitting shows its room or remote format, the agenda items and, when available:
  * **Discussed prints:** — each **Print** chip opens the print in [Legislation](/legislation-tracker);
  * **Recording:** — **Open recording** opens the sitting recording in [Recordings](/live-proceedings); a sitting with several recordings lists them by number.

The right-hand panel (on a phone, the **Details** button) shows **Details** — **Type**, **Code**, **Members**, **Prints**, **Sittings** and **Latest sitting** — and **Contents** with links to both sections.

Committee pages also open from Advisor citations and from links to committees elsewhere in Persate.

## Empty and error states

* *No committees found* — no committee matches the search or the type; change the search or the filters.
* *Couldn't load committees* — the committee list failed to load.
* *Could not load Sejm members.* — the members list failed to load.
* *No sittings recorded* — no sittings of the committee have been registered yet.
* *Committee not found* — the committee could not be loaded; it may have been removed or the link may be outdated.
