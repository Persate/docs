---
title: Events and stories
description: Follow events on a timeline or on a board of categories, read event details with representative posts, and open story pages.
---

**Events** is the default tab of **Media**. Persate groups posts into **events** — concrete developments discussed in the posts — and groups related but distinct events into **stories**. Each story belongs to one of six categories: **Security**, **Law & government**, **Energy & climate**, **Economy**, **International** and **Public life**.

Two buttons next to the tabs switch the display between **Timeline view** (the default) and **Story graph view**. Both follow the **Timeline range**, **Custom period**, **Categories** and **Sort events** filters described in [Media](/public-pulse).

## Timeline view

Each row is a story. The left column shows the category icon and the title; when a story groups several events, the story title is followed by the number of events. Hovering over a title shows it in full.

The right part is a time axis. A range of two days or more is divided into days, with weekends shaded; a shorter range shows hours. The view opens at the most recent activity. Scroll sideways to go back in time and down to see more stories; *Loading more events…* appears while further stories load.

* Each bar is an event and spans the period of its activity. Overlapping events of the same story are stacked in separate lanes, and the story's newest event is highlighted.
* Dots inside a bar mark individual posts; posts from official sources stand out in a separate color.

To open details:

* select a bar or a dot to open **Event details** for that event;
* select a story title: a story with several events opens its story page, and a story with a single event opens **Event details**.

## Story graph view

The board has one column per category, with the number of stories in it; when **Categories** is set, only the selected categories appear. Each card is a story. Its title opens the story page, and the compact timeline beneath it lists the story's events with date and time; selecting an event opens **Event details**. Drag the board sideways to reach further columns. *No stories in this category.* marks an empty column.

## Event details

The **Event details** panel shows the event title, its summary and three figures:

* **Sources** — the number of independent sources;
* **Posts** — the number of posts assigned to the event;
* **Confidence** — the event's confidence score, as a percentage.

**Representative evidence** lists the most representative posts, with posts from official sources first and marked **Official**. Each card shows the author, the text and the reactions; **Open post on X ↗** opens the original post. *Evidence projection is still processing.* means the posts for the event are not available yet.

In the timeline and on the board, **Open story** goes to the story page. **Close**, or a click outside the panel, closes it.

## Story page

A story page, marked *Event story*, shows the story title and description, followed by these sections:

* **Activity** — post volume over the duration of the story.
* **Events** — a card for each event, newest first, with its status (**open**, **quiet** or **closed**), the time of the latest activity, the summary, the number of posts and sources, and the confidence. **Open details** opens **Event details**.
* **Active people** — the people posting in the story, with **Club**, **Posts** and **Engagement**.
* **Engagement** — **Reactions**: totals of **Replies**, **Reposts**, **Likes**, **Quotes** and **Views**, each with the average per post.
* **Posts** — the story's posts as cards.

The right-hand panel (on a phone, the **Details** button) shows **At a glance** — **Window**, **Events**, **Posts**, **Sources** and **Active people** — and **Reactions**. The **Events** breadcrumb returns to the timeline.

If the story is no longer available, *Story unavailable* appears with **Open event timeline**.

## Links from Advisor

Advisor answers can cite media stories and events. A cited story opens its story page. A cited event opens its story page with **Event details** for that event already open. When the event cannot be opened, *Media event unavailable* appears.
