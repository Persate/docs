**Recordings** gives access to video recordings of Sejm proceedings — plenary sittings, committee and subcommittee sittings, conferences and other events — together with Persate's analysis of each recording: a transcript with identified speakers and, for finished recordings, a timeline, a polished transcript and the official Sejm transcript where these are available.

The module appears under **PUBLIC → Recordings** when the feature is enabled for the organization. Otherwise the page shows **This feature is not included in your plan**.

## Ongoing, Previous and Scheduled

The top bar switches between three lists:

* **Ongoing** — proceedings being broadcast right now.
* **Previous** — proceedings that have ended. This list opens by default.
* **Scheduled** — announced proceedings that have not started yet.

**Ongoing** and **Previous** show the newest proceedings first; **Scheduled** shows the nearest first. A proceeding is listed in only one of the three lists and moves to the next one when it starts and ends.

## List and tiles

The toggle at the right of the top bar switches between two layouts. The browser remembers the choice.

* **List** — rows grouped by month, with the columns **Recording**, **Date**, **Type** and **Status**.
* **Tiles** — one card per recording with its first frame, type, start time and room.

Selecting a row or a tile opens the recording; see [Opening a proceeding](/live-proceedings/analysis).

## Status icons

The **Status** column — in tiles, the top-right corner of the card — shows at most one icon per recording. Hovering over the icon shows what it means.

| Icon | Meaning |
|---|---|
| No icon | The recording has no analysis yet. |
| Check mark | **Recording Analysis** is complete. |
| Spinning circle | The analysis is running or queued; while it runs, the tooltip shows the percentage done. |
| Red cross | The analysis failed, or it stopped and is waiting to resume on its own. The tooltip names the state — for example *retry scheduled* or *waiting for recording* — and, when known, the time of the next retry or check. |
| Grey question mark | **No transcript · Needs verification** — the analysis finished but saved no transcript. This does not confirm that nobody spoke; the analysis can be run again from the recording view. |
| Grey circle | The original video is no longer available from its source: **Persate only** (Persate plays the copy it saved) or **Video unavailable**. Any saved analysis remains accessible. |

[Analysis status](/live-proceedings/status) explains each state and the action it offers.

## Narrowing the list

The side panel filters the list that is currently open:

* **Search** — matches the title, the committee and the room (*Search sessions...*).
* **Date Range** — limits the list to a selected period.
* **Proceeding type** — **Session**, **Commission**, **Subcommittee**, **Conference** or **Other**; several types can be selected at once.
* **Recording availability** — shows only recordings marked **Persate only**, **Unavailable** or **Stale unavailable**. With nothing selected (**Any availability**), all recordings are listed.
* **Political party** — shows recordings whose title names a parliamentary club or party, such as a club's press conference.

The selected filters stay in place when switching between the lists and are kept for the rest of the browser session.

## Other ways to open a recording

Recordings can also be opened from other parts of Persate, usually at the relevant moment:

* **Search** — results include recordings and matching fragments of their transcripts; a transcript match opens the recording at that moment. See [Search and notifications](/using-persate/search-and-notifications).
* **Advisor** — a citation of a statement in an Advisor answer opens the recording at the cited moment.
* **Tracker** — alert matches found in Sejm speeches offer a **Play from** link to the matched moment.
* **Legislation** — committee pages link to the recordings of their sittings (**Open recording**).

## Empty and error states

* **No proceedings found** — the list is empty or no recording in it matches the active filters.
* **Failed to load proceedings** — the list could not be loaded; trying again later is recommended.
