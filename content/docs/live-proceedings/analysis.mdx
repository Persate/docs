---
title: Opening a proceeding
description: The recording view — the player, a transcript that follows playback, the Raw, Polished, Timeline and Official transcript views, the Advisor summary, and live analysis.
---

Selecting a recording in the list opens the recording view. It combines the video player with the analysis panel, and the two stay in sync: the transcript follows playback, and selecting a fragment of the transcript moves the video to that moment. Links that point to a moment — from Search, Advisor citations or alert matches — open the view at that moment; the transcript is positioned there even when the video itself cannot be played.

## View layout

* **Left** — the player; below it the title, date, type, room and description of the proceeding, followed by the **Advisor summary** card when one is available.
* **Right** — the analysis panel with the transcript. Dragging the divider between the columns changes the panel's width; the browser remembers it.
* **Right edge** — a rail of views: **Raw**, **Polished**, **Timeline** and, when the Sejm has published one for the recording, **Official transcript**. Views that are not available yet are greyed out.

On a narrow screen the transcript sits below the player. The **Details** button on the player opens the type, date, room, description and timeline chapters, and the switch above the transcript chooses between **Enhanced** (the polished transcript) and **Raw**.

## Player

* The bar along the bottom of the video plays and pauses, moves through the recording, mutes the sound and switches to full screen.
* **Faces** in the top-left corner turns the face overlays on and off (**Faces off**). The overlays name the people recognised in the frame; the browser remembers the setting.
* During a broadcast, **Live** in the top-right corner marks the live edge. After rewinding it changes to **Go Live**, which returns to the live edge.
* When a recording starts before the proceedings do, **Skip to start** — with the time in brackets — appears below the player and moves to the moment the content begins.
* A scheduled proceeding shows **Proceeding Scheduled** with the date from which the recording will be available.
* When the original video is no longer available from its source, a badge above the player says so; see [Analysis status](/live-proceedings/status).

## Transcript

The **Raw** view shows the complete transcript of the recording:

* Statements are grouped by speaker and carry their time range. A check mark next to a name marks a verified identity; the name and photo link to the person's stakeholder profile, which opens in a new tab.
* A speaker who introduced themselves but has not been verified carries a badge with the stated role (*Self-introduced — identity unverified*). Where Persate's own attribution is not verified, a badge can show the speaker named in the official Sejm transcript; this is not a verified identity either.
* Speakers who are not recognised are shown as **Unknown Speaker** or under a provisional label such as *Speaker 1*.
* The fragment being spoken is highlighted. Selecting a fragment — or a single word — moves the video to that moment.
* A statement made in a foreign language is followed by its Polish translation (*Translation in preparation* while the translation is being prepared).
* The button at the bottom of the panel controls automatic scrolling. **Following** keeps the current fragment in view; selecting it switches automatic scrolling off (**Auto-scroll off**), and selecting it again switches it back on. After manual scrolling, **Jump to latest** returns to the current fragment.

## Polished transcript

**Polished** shows an edited version of the transcript, organised into readable passages by speaker. It is easier to read and suits quoting and further analytical work. The view becomes available once the polished version has been generated for the recording (*Polishing transcript…* while it is being prepared); until then it is greyed out and the panel shows **Raw**.

## Timeline

**Timeline** divides the proceeding into successive topics. Each entry shows the start time, the duration, a title, a short summary and keywords; selecting an entry moves the video and the transcript to the start of that topic. The timeline is available once it has been generated (*Building timeline…*); until then the view shows **No Timeline Yet**.

## Official Sejm transcript

When the Sejm has published an official transcript (stenogram) of a plenary or committee sitting, a strip above the transcript names the sitting (*Official Sejm transcript: …*) and **Open transcript** opens it. The **Official transcript** view in the rail opens it as well.

* The header shows the sitting, a **Source** link to the published original and how many of its statements were matched to the recording.
* A matched statement carries its time; selecting the time plays the recording from that statement. The statement being played is highlighted.
* A statement that could not be matched says why: **Attendance list** (collapsed), **Submitted in writing, not spoken**, **Covers the whole sitting day** or **Not matched to the recording**.
* Speaker names and the text appear exactly as published, without translation.

## Advisor summary

Below the player, the **Advisor summary** card gives an editorial title and an overview of the course of the proceeding — the main threads, points of contention and outcomes. It belongs to the analysis of a finished recording and appears only when a summary has been generated. The card is not shown on narrow screens.

## Live and finished recordings

Persate analyses a recording in one of two tiers. The view selects the tier on its own; there is no manual switch:

| Tier | When | What it provides |
|---|---|---|
| **Record Analysis** | During the broadcast (**Ongoing**; the proceeding is marked **Live Session**) | A transcript that grows as the proceeding goes on, with recognised speakers. |
| **Enhanced Record** | After the proceeding (**Previous**) | The complete transcript with speakers and, when generated, **Polished**, **Timeline** and the **Advisor summary**; the official Sejm transcript when published. |

In the interface both tiers appear as **Recording Analysis**. Opening an ongoing proceeding connects to the broadcast and starts the live analysis automatically. A placeholder at the end of the transcript shows that the next section is being analysed, and hovering over the player shows how far the analysis has reached (**Analysis**). Until the full analysis of a finished recording is published, the view keeps showing the last saved transcript from the broadcast.

## Recommended workflow

1. Check **Ongoing** for proceedings being broadcast and **Scheduled** for upcoming ones.
2. Open an ongoing proceeding to follow the statements and speakers as the session goes on.
3. After the proceeding, open it from **Previous** and use **Timeline** or **Official transcript** to find the part of interest.
4. Select a fragment of the transcript to play the exact statement.
5. To find statements across many recordings, use **Search** or ask **Advisor** — their links open the recording at the right moment.

When the panel shows a status instead of the transcript, [Analysis status](/live-proceedings/status) explains what it means and what can be done.
