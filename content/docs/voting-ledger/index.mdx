---
title: Votes
description: Browse Sejm and Senate votes, presidential decisions, and Constitutional Tribunal cases.
---

Open **PUBLIC → Votes**. The top navigation separates four public institutional records:

* **Sejm** — vote outcomes and per-member voting records;
* **Senat** — Senate vote outcomes and, where published, per-senator voting records;
* **Prezydent** — presidential decisions on legislation; these are decisions, not chamber votes;
* **TK** — Constitutional Tribunal cases and rulings; these are cases and judgments, not votes.

Do not combine metrics across tabs. Each institution publishes different fields and follows a different decision process.

## Sejm

The Sejm table shows the result, motion, topic, vote distribution, margin, date, and proceeding/vote number. Select a row to open the complete record.

**Search current page** filters records already loaded by title, topic, or number. The **Procedural votes in Ledger** preference controls whether agenda changes, recesses, quorum checks, and similar procedural votes are shown; they are hidden by default.

Result labels can include **Passed**, **Rejected**, **Quorum present**, **Quorum missing**, and list-vote outcomes. The recorded majority rule determines the result where applicable.

## Senat

The Senate table presents the official Senate result, subject, topic, distribution, margin, date, and vote number made available by the source. Search filters the records already fetched. Select a row to open its details.

Senate records are not inferred from the Sejm record. If the source has not published a field or a member list, Persate leaves that information unavailable.

## Prezydent

**Awaiting decision** lists acts sent to the President during the current Sejm term, with their decision deadline and available Sejm support. Joint prints appear as one act. The badge on the President tab shows the number awaiting a recorded decision. Select an act to open its legislative process.

**Recorded decisions** groups decisions by day. Vetoes and referrals show their subsequent Sejm or Tribunal status when available; signatures appear as compact buttons. Select a decision to open its detail. A signature and same-day referral of the same act appear together. Search filters loaded decisions and the awaiting acts; the **Outcome** filter narrows recorded decisions.

The President acts individually, so Persate does not show a vote distribution or chamber map for this tab.

## TK

Pending cases appear before rulings. Pending cases show their waiting time; preventive cases carry an **Act on hold** label. Rulings show their outcome and, when the ruling text names them, the judges on the bench. Orange seats mark separate opinions.

**Search all cases** searches stored signatures, subjects, applicants, and challenged references. Use **Status** to choose pending cases or rulings, and **Case type** to filter by signature family. Each section has its own **Load more** control.

Select a case to open its record and related official material.

## Data and empty states

Persate presents fields published by the relevant public source. Missing values are shown as unavailable; they are not estimated.

* No results after search — clear the current-page query and institution-specific filters.
* Source unavailable — retry after refreshing; keep the institution and record number when reporting the problem.
* Detail unavailable — the public source may not expose the record yet, or the identifier may no longer resolve.
