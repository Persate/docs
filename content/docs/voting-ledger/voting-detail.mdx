---
title: Record details
description: Read institution-specific voting, decision, and case records without conflating their data.
---

Open a record from **PUBLIC → Votes**. The available sections depend on the active institution and on what its official source publishes.

## Sejm voting detail

The Sejm detail page can show:

* result and threshold information;
* voting time, attendance, turnout, and majority rule;
* counts by option;
* related official items and documents;
* a seat-by-seat chamber map with member votes.

Use the club filter above the chamber map to focus on one parliamentary club; **All seats** restores the full map. Select or hover a seat where supported to inspect the member and recorded vote.

## Senate voting detail

The Senate detail page uses the official Senate result and available counts. It can include related documents, linked legislative processes, and a Senate chamber distribution when the source provides member votes that can be matched to seats.

An absent senator list or chamber visualization means that the required source data is unavailable; Persate does not substitute Sejm data.

## Presidential decisions

The detail opens with the decision and the act it concerns. Available sections include the automated overview, the act's legislative process, veto justification, the Sejm's reconsideration, linked Tribunal cases, earlier vetoes of an act with the same name, and source documents.

For a veto reconsideration, the page shows the recorded vote and the three-fifths threshold. The **Path of the act** panel connects recorded Sejm, Senate, President, and Tribunal events, with links to available records. Repeated print references and explicitly labelled accessible/scanned versions are combined; the accessible version is preferred.

## Constitutional Tribunal case detail

The TK detail uses the same page layout, with the outcome first and the **Path of the act** panel beside it. It can show the automated overview, named bench, unanimity or majority, separate opinions, a stated loss-of-force date, the original ruling text, and sources. Separate opinions can concern the reasoning or panel composition and do not establish an exact vote tally.

A case may exist before a ruling is published; in that state, verdict information remains unavailable. Source text stays in its original language when the interface language changes.

## Documents and provenance

Related items may open in a Persate document panel or in a new browser tab, depending on the source and format. Treat the linked official document as the authoritative text. A missing document is displayed as unavailable and is not recreated by Persate.

## Troubleshooting

* Record cannot be loaded — return to the institution list and open it again; the source may be temporarily unavailable.
* No related documents — the source has not linked any documents to this record.
* No chamber map — the institution has no chamber vote, or seat-level source data is unavailable.
* Counts differ from a later official publication — record the URL and time and report the discrepancy; public-source corrections can arrive after the first publication.
