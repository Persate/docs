Every Persate module sits in the same frame: the sidebar on the left, the header with help and account controls at the top, and the module in the main area. On a phone, a dock at the bottom of the screen replaces both. The entries shown depend on the features enabled for the organization, the user's role and the Labs assigned to the user.

## Sidebar

Two actions sit at the top of the sidebar:

* **Upload** — opens the upload panel for Repository files. It is shown when the Repository is enabled for the organization.
* **Search** — opens global search. See [Search and notifications](/using-persate/search-and-notifications).

The navigation below them is grouped as follows:

| Group | Entries |
|---|---|
| — | **[Cockpit](/cockpit)** — the start screen |
| **CORE** | **[Advisor](/advisor)**, **[Tracker](/alerts)** |
| **WORKSPACE** | **[Repository](/repository)**, **[Smart Folders](/repository/smart-folders)**, **[Editor](/editor)** |
| **PUBLIC** | **[Legislation](/legislation-tracker)**, **[Recordings](/live-proceedings)**, **[Votes](/voting-ledger)**, **[Stakeholders](/stakeholders)**, **[Media](/public-pulse)** |
| **Labs** | the [Labs](/labs) assigned to the user |

An entry appears only when its feature is enabled for the organization, and a group without entries is hidden. **Labs** is a collapsible section at the bottom of the sidebar; it appears only when at least one Lab is assigned and remembers whether it was left expanded. A page opened directly when the organization's plan does not include it shows **This feature is not included in your plan**. Hidden entries are a convenience only: access is checked again whenever data loads.

The button at the left end of the header collapses the sidebar to a rail of icons and expands it again; Ctrl+B (⌘B on a Mac) does the same. Inside a module, a bar above the page shows the group and the module and, on a detail page, the path to the open record; the module name then links back to the module's main page.

In a browser on Windows or macOS, **Download** at the bottom of the sidebar downloads the Persate desktop app. In the desktop app for Windows, **Update ready** appears there when a new version is ready; selecting it restarts Persate to install the update.

## Header

The left side of the header holds the sidebar button, the help button, the **Go back** and **Go forward** arrows and the Persate wordmark. On a page that has a guide, the help button is **Guide & FAQ** and opens that page's guide (see [Interactive tutorials](../account-and-settings/tutorials)); elsewhere it is **Open documentation**, which opens this documentation in a new tab in the interface language.

The right side holds:

* the theme switch, which toggles between light and dark and saves the choice to the account; **Settings → Preferences → Appearance** also offers following the system setting;
* **Feedback**;
* **Announcements** and **Messages** — two feeds with unread counters, described in [Search and notifications](/using-persate/search-and-notifications);
* the account menu (the avatar), which shows the display name and email address and offers **Account settings**, **Preferences** and **Log out**.

Notices from the Persate team can appear as bars above the header, and a bar also appears when the connection is lost or data loads unusually slowly. See [Search and notifications](/using-persate/search-and-notifications).

## Sending feedback

**Feedback** sends a rating, comments and screenshots to the Persate team.

1. Select **Feedback** in the header.
2. Optionally add screenshots under **Upload visuals**: drag them in, pick files or paste them with Ctrl+V. PNG and JPG images are accepted, up to ten.
3. Choose a rating from 0 to 5 under **How was your experience?** A rating is required.
4. Describe the experience under **Your feedback** and select **Submit feedback**.

## Settings

The account menu opens **Account settings** or **Preferences** directly. While settings are open, the sidebar lists the settings pages instead of the modules:

* **PERSONAL** — **Account**, **Security & Privacy**, **Notifications**, **Preferences**;
* **PLATFORM** — **Sources**;
* **ORGANIZATION** — **General**, **Members**, **Groups & permissions**, **Usage & plan**, **External providers**, **Experimental features**. **Members**, **Groups & permissions** and **Usage & plan** are listed only for organization administrators.

**Back to app** at the top of the settings sidebar returns to the last page visited, or to the Cockpit. See [Account and settings](/account-and-settings), [Sources](/sources) and [Administration](/administration).

## On a phone

On a phone, the sidebar and the header are replaced by a dock at the bottom of the screen. It holds buttons for the Cockpit, search, upload (when the Repository is enabled) and the profile, which opens Settings, plus a menu button that lists every available module. On conversation screens such as Advisor, the dock shrinks to a single menu button so that it does not cover the message field.

* Page filters open from a filter button as a panel over the page.
* In Settings, the top bar shows **Back to app**, the page name and **Sections**, which opens the list of settings pages.
* Announcements, Messages and Feedback are in the header, which is shown on larger screens.
* Persate on a phone works in portrait orientation. In landscape it shows **Portrait mode only** until the phone is turned upright.

## Access messages

* **This feature is not included in your plan** — the organization's plan does not include the module. Contact an organization administrator to extend the plan.
* **Access Denied** — the account is not assigned to a Persate organization, or an account without the administrator role opened organization management pages. An invited user should open the invitation link from the email; otherwise ask an organization administrator for an invitation or a role change.
* **Sign-in check unavailable** — Persate could not confirm the session and loaded no data. Select **Try again** after a moment.
