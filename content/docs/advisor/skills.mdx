title: Advisor skills
description: Description of the advisor's skills, scope of operation, typical questions, and returned resources.
---

The **AI Advisor's** capabilities are divided into six **skill families**, each exposing a set of analytical tools. Every tool call appears in the message timeline, identifying the executed action.

## Legislation

Analysis of the parliamentary activity register: votes, motions, resolutions, and related data.

**Typical questions:**
* *"What was the result of the last vote on the energy bill?"*
* *"How did the budget committee resolve amendment no. 17?"*
* *"List the votes from the last Sejm session that passed with a margin of less than five votes."*

**Returned resources:**
* A link to the vote, opening in the **Voting ledger**.

## Stakeholders

Searching the stakeholder catalog: biographies, clubs, districts, voting attendance, social media handles, and identified speech fragments.

**Typical questions:**
* *"Who chairs the Public Finances Committee and what is their voting record on tax matters?"*
* *"Find every stakeholder from the Greater Poland voivodeship who voted against the climate bill."*
* *"What did MP Nowak say in the plenum about the housing reform?"*

**Returned resources:**
* A link to the stakeholder profile.

## Public pulse

Searching the aggregated X (Twitter) stream covering all monitored stakeholders.

**Typical questions:**
* *"What has the opposition said publicly about the latest amendment to the labor code?"*
* *"Has any minister posted about the budget vote since it passed?"*
* *"Highlight the trending hashtags from stakeholder posts in the last 48 hours."*

**Returned resources:**
* A link to the original post on X.

## Alerts

Managing existing alerts and creating new rules.

**Typical questions:**
* *"Which alerts triggered most frequently this week?"*
* *"Configure an alert for any mention of the new pharmaceutical bill."*
* *"Pause my alert regarding Brexit references — the topic is no longer relevant."*

**Returned resources:**
* A link to the detailed alert view.

## Documents

Searching and performing operations on files from the **Repository**.

**Typical questions:**
* *"What does our internal briefing say about the gas pricing mechanism?"*
* *"Search every government white paper for references to industrial subsidies."*
* *"Find the fragment where the public procurement bill defines 'critical infrastructure'."*
* *"How many documents have we uploaded since the beginning of the year?"*

**Returned resources:**
* A link to the specific file or its fragment.

## Chat context

Searching within previous conversations with the advisor.

**Typical questions:**
* *"We discussed the energy bill earlier today — return to that thread."*
* *"What did you tell me last week about the public procurement reform?"*

**Returned resources:**
* A link to the archived message or conversation.

## How the advisor selects tools

The advisor selects tools based on the form of the question. Their selection is automatic and does not require explicit indication by the user. Typical patterns:

* A question mentioning a specific stakeholder — almost always `Stakeholders`.
* A question about *public statements* — `Public pulse`.
* A question about *voting patterns* — `Legislation`.
* A question about a document — `Documents`.
* Multi-part questions — combining multiple tools.

When a question extends beyond the available tools, the advisor answers what it has tools for and notes the unanswered part.
