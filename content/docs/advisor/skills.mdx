The **Advisor** reaches the platform's data through the MCP layer. Its tools are grouped into **skill families**; each family is a namespace of tools for one area of the platform, and every tool call appears in the activity as a chip with the family's icon. The tools available depend on the modules enabled for the organization.

## Skill families

| Family | Scope |
| :--- | :--- |
| `legislation` | Sejm and Senate votes and how individual MPs voted, presidential decisions, Constitutional Tribunal cases, the civic register of people and organizations, and searches across acts and publications of public institutions. |
| `civic` | Legal acts in depth — text, structure, amendments, consolidated texts and implementing acts; Sejm prints and legislative processes; RCL projects; interpellations and written questions; committees and sittings; voting analyses by topic. |
| `stakeholders` | Profiles of politicians and organizations, MP search by club or constituency, contact details and activity. |
| `public_pulse` | Media: archived X posts of tracked accounts, events, stories, topics, trends and deleted posts. |
| `recordings` | Analyzed recordings: catalog, summaries, transcripts, speeches and the official stenogram. |
| `documents`, `files` | Repository files and the public Persate corpus: content, filename and sequence search, metadata, summaries, counts and full-file reading. |
| `events` | Developments over a period: changes across public sources and new developments in the organization's files. |
| `alerts` | The user's alerts and their matches; alert drafts, changes and deletion on request. |
| `smartfolders` | Preparing a Smart Folder for the Smart Folders creator. |
| `web_research` | Web search and reading public web pages. |
| `chat_context` | The user's earlier Advisor conversations. |
| `workspace`, `team`, `notifications` | Workspace summary and personal preferences, the team directory, and the notification inbox. |
| `labs` | Reviewed lab releases, when Labs are enabled for the organization. |
| `agent` | The Advisor's own steps: plan updates, clarifying questions, research agents, tool lookup and preparing drafts in other modules. |

## Votes and parliamentary records (`legislation`)

The structured record of parliamentary and constitutional activity: Sejm and Senate votes, their results and how individual MPs voted, presidential decisions and Constitutional Tribunal cases, together with the civic register of people and organizations.

**Typical questions:**
* *"What was the result of the last vote on the energy bill?"*
* *"How did the Senate vote on the latest amendment to the energy law?"*
* *"List the votes from the last Sejm sitting that passed by fewer than five votes."*
* *"Which Constitutional Tribunal cases concern the act on the National Council of the Judiciary?"*

**Citations open:** the Sejm or Senate vote in **Votes**.

## Legal acts and legislative documents (`civic`)

Published acts (Dziennik Ustaw, Monitor Polski) with their structure, amendments, consolidated texts and implementing acts; Sejm prints and the course of legislative processes; RCL projects; interpellations and written questions with government replies; committees and their sittings; analyses of how clubs and MPs voted on a topic.

**Typical questions:**
* *"What does Article 5 of the public procurement act say, and when was it last amended?"*
* *"At what stage is the bill in Sejm print 1234, and what happened at the last vote?"*
* *"Which interpellations about hospital debt are still awaiting a reply from the Ministry of Health?"*

**Citations open:** the act or the exact cited passage in the **Legislation** reader, a print document, an RCL project, a parliamentary question or a committee page.

## Stakeholders (`stakeholders`)

Profiles of politicians and organizations: biography, club, constituency, voting attendance, contact details, social media accounts and activity.

**Typical questions:**
* *"Who chairs the Public Finances Committee, and what is their voting record on tax matters?"*
* *"Find every MP from the Greater Poland voivodeship who voted against the climate bill."*
* *"Prepare a short brief on MP Nowak before a meeting: club, committees, recent activity."*

**Citations open:** the stakeholder profile.

## Media (`public_pulse`)

The archive of X posts published by the accounts tracked in Persate, grouped in the Media module into events, stories and topics.

**Typical questions:**
* *"What has the opposition said publicly about the latest amendment to the labor code?"*
* *"Has any minister posted about the budget vote since it passed?"*
* *"Show the trending hashtags in posts by tracked accounts over the last 48 hours."*

**Citations open:** the original post on X, or a Media event or story.

## Recordings (`recordings`)

Analyzed recordings of Sejm sittings, committees and other proceedings: summaries, transcripts, identified speeches and the official stenogram.

**Typical questions:**
* *"What did MP Nowak say in the plenary about the housing reform?"*
* *"Summarize the latest sitting of the Health Committee."*
* *"Which of the latest recordings have already been analyzed?"*

**Citations open:** the recording in **Recordings** at the cited moment, or the exact transcript passage.

## Documents (`documents`, `files`)

Search and operations on files from the **Repository** and on the public Persate corpus: content, filename and sequence search, file metadata and summaries, counts, and reading a whole file.

**Typical questions:**
* *"What does our internal briefing say about the gas pricing mechanism?"*
* *"Find the passage where our draft position defines 'critical infrastructure'."*
* *"How many documents have we uploaded since the beginning of the year?"*

**Citations open:** the file in the file viewer, at the cited passage when there is one.

## Alerts (`alerts`)

The user's alerts and their matches, preparing new alerts and changing existing ones. A new alert is prepared as an inactive draft that opens in the Tracker's alert creator for review; it starts working only after it is saved there. Changing, pausing or deleting an existing alert happens only on an explicit request, and deletion asks for confirmation in the conversation.

**Typical questions:**
* *"Which of my alerts matched most often this week?"*
* *"Prepare an alert for any mention of the new pharmaceutical bill."*
* *"Pause my alert about Brexit references — the topic is no longer relevant."*

**Citations open:** the alert detail in the **Tracker**.

## Conversation history (`chat_context`)

Searching and reading the user's earlier conversations with the Advisor.

**Typical questions:**
* *"We discussed the energy bill earlier today — pick that thread up again."*
* *"What did you tell me last week about the public procurement reform?"*

**Citations open:** the earlier conversation.

## Web research (`web_research`)

Searching the web and reading public pages, when a question asks for it or needs public context the platform does not hold. Pages found or read appear as web source chips in the activity; web results are not stored in the platform.

**Typical questions:**
* *"Read this ministry announcement and summarize the changes it proposes: (link)"*
* *"Search the web for the latest official statement on the energy price cap."*

**Citations open:** the web page in a new tab.

## Other families

* **`events`** — what changed in a period across the platform's public sources, or what new developments were found in the organization's files. Example: *"What changed this week on energy policy across legislation and recordings?"*
* **`smartfolders`** — prepares a Smart Folder from a description and opens the Smart Folders creator with the request filled in. Example: *"Create a Smart Folder with all our documents on the energy transition."*
* **`workspace`**, **`team`**, **`notifications`** — the workspace summary and personal preferences, the team directory and the notification inbox. Example: *"Which notifications have I not read yet?"*
* **`labs`** — reviewed lab releases, when Labs are enabled for the organization. See [Labs](/labs).
* **`agent`** — the Advisor's own steps: updating the plan, asking a clarifying question, starting research agents, looking up a tool, and preparing a draft for the Tracker or the Editor.

## Citation URIs

Every citation in an answer is a resource URI that the interface turns into a chip. The table lists the URI schemes and what each chip opens.

| URI | Opens |
| :--- | :--- |
| `legislation://voting/{sitting}/{vote}` | Sejm vote in **Votes** |
| `legislation://senate-voting/{vote_id}` | Senate vote in **Votes** |
| `legislation://act/{ELI}` | Published act in the **Legislation** reader, e.g. `legislation://act/DU/2017/59` |
| `legislation://chunk/{chunk_id}/{offset}` | Cited passage of a legal text, in the reader at that passage |
| `legislation://print/{term}/{number}` | Sejm print document; a print with several documents shows a list to choose from |
| `legislation://document/{ELI}` | Print attachment or other legislative document in the reader |
| `legislation://rcl_project/{project_id}` | RCL project in **Legislation** |
| `legislation://rcl_document/{project_id}/{document_id}` | RCL project with that document's preview open |
| `legislation://question/{term}/{type}/{number}` | Interpellation or written question, at the reply when one is cited |
| `legislation://committee/{code}` | Sejm committee page in **Stakeholders** |
| `feature://stakeholder/{stakeholder_id}` | Stakeholder profile |
| `feature://public_pulse/tweet/{tweet_id}` | Original post on X (new tab) |
| `feature://public_pulse/event/{event_id}` | Media event |
| `feature://public_pulse/story/{story_id}` | Media story |
| `feature://alert/{alert_id}` | Alert detail in the **Tracker** |
| `feature://recording-transcript/{recording}/{segment}/{start}/{end}` | Exact transcript passage on a separate page |
| `recordings://speech/{recording}/{seconds}` | Recording in **Recordings** at the cited moment |
| `recordings://official/{source}/{file}` | Official Sejm stenogram (new tab) |
| `documents://file/{file_id}` | File in the file viewer |
| `documents://chunk/{file_id}/{chunk_id}` | File at the cited passage, with **Cited passage** shown |
| `documents://chunk-page/{file_id}/{chunk_id}/{start}/{end}` | File at an exact character range of a passage |
| `web://fetch/{evidence_id}` | Web page (new tab) |
| `chatcontext://session/{session_id}` | Earlier Advisor conversation |

A citation that cannot be resolved stays in the answer as plain text.

## How the Advisor selects tools

The Advisor selects tools based on the form of the question; the selection is automatic and requires no indication by the user. For specialized tasks, chips such as `Looking up a tool` or `Loading a skill` show the Advisor finding the right tools first. Typical patterns:

* A question naming a specific MP — `stakeholders`, often together with `legislation`.
* A question about *public statements* — `public_pulse`.
* A question about *how someone voted* — `legislation`.
* A question about the wording or history of a law — `civic`.
* A question about *what was said* at a sitting — `recordings`.
* A question about a document — `documents`.
* Multi-part questions — several families combined.

When a question extends beyond the available tools, the Advisor answers the part it has tools for and notes the part left unanswered.
